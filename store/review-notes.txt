Games 4 All is a fully offline collection of 60 short learning games for children and families. There is no sign-in, no account, no network access, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchase. No sign-in or demo account is needed to review it.

PARENTAL GATE
Parent Zone (profiles, settings, play-time limits, the privacy policy and "Delete all data") is behind a grown-ups-only question. It shows a multiplication such as 7 × 93: type the answer (651) on the number pad. A wrong answer brings a new question.

FIRST LAUNCH
Tap "Let's set this up" (the same question) to make a profile, or "Skip for now" to start straight away.

PLAY-TIME LIMITS
In Parent Zone, set a limit per sitting or per day. If a limit is reached in the middle of a round, that round may finish (for up to three minutes, marked LAST ROUND in the game's header); then a break screen appears. A sitting ends after ten minutes without play, and the break screen clears itself then.

SPORTS QUOTES
Each game shows a short quotation from a well-known athlete, with their name, as encouragement. No endorsement by them is implied.

PRIVACY
The app collects no data at all; everything it keeps stays on the device. The privacy policy is in Parent Zone → "Read the privacy policy", and at the privacy URL on this listing.
